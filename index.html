      About the Ontario alignment. Connections are mapped at the strand and topic level to The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 1–8: Science and Technology (2022), Grade 9 Science SNC1W (2022) and Grade 10 Science SNC2D (2008). Direct means the activity teaches content named in that strand; Supporting means it reinforces the strand's skills or ideas. Teachers should confirm specific expectations for their grade.
